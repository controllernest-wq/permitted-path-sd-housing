# Roadblocks — named and pre-addressed

## Financing and occupancy

| Roadblock | Why it kills the plan | Pre-address |
|---|---|---|
| Occupancy fraud | Federal crime; repurchase; due-on issues | Real move-in within 60 days; 12-month stay on FHA; paper trail |
| SDHC / County DCCA used on a 12-month flip-to-rental | Notes accelerate when you stop occupying | Do not use these programs unless you will stay for the life of the silent second *or* you can repay them from equity |
| CalHFA MyHome on a duplex | MyHome property pages emphasize 1-unit | Use MyHome on SFR+ADU; use GSFA/FHA on 2–4 unit |
| Chenoa forgivable product + early move-out | Forgiveness often needs 36 on-time payments and occupancy | Prefer repayable DPA if month-13 move is certain |
| FHA 3–4 unit self-sufficiency test | Deal dies in underwriting | Run appraiser-style rent test before offering |
| FHA condo not on approved list | Cannot close FHA | Check roster before falling in love with the building |
| One FHA at a time | Blocks “buy the next one at month 6” | Sequence: occupy 12 months, then next owner-oc loan |
| DTI after residual income + CA taxes | Good credit ≠ approval | Get a Full File Credit Review + AUS findings first |
| Reserves | Low-cash buyers fail here more than on down payment | Keep 3–6 months PITI unspent |
| Gift-fund seasoning / source | Underwriter conditions | Paper trail from donor account |

## Tax and exit

| Roadblock | Why it kills the plan | Pre-address |
|---|---|---|
| Section 121 at month 12 | 24-month use test not met | If selling is the goal, stay 24 months; if 12 is the goal, underwrite as a *taxable* sale or keep as rental |
| Depreciation recapture | Even with 121, rental-portion depreciation is recaptured | Track basis from day one; CPA before sale |
| California state tax on gains | CA does not give a special low CG rate | Model 13.3% state on taxable gain |
| 1031 on a primary | 1031 is for property held for investment | Live-in year is not a 1031 setup; after conversion and investment intent, maybe later — attorney required |
| Prop 13 / reassessment | New purchase resets assessed value | Already priced into holding cost; not a surprise |

## Property and operations (California)

| Roadblock | Why it kills the plan | Pre-address |
|---|---|---|
| Unpermitted unit | Cannot legally rent; insurance gap; FHA repair list | Demand permit history from City/County DSD |
| Tenant already in place + certain DPA | Relocation rules; ineligible property | Prefer owner-occupied or 30+ day vacant if using those programs |
| Costa-Hawkins / local rent rules | Limits future rent on some units | Know the ordinance for that city before you buy |
| Just-cause eviction | You cannot casually “need the unit back” | Use a manager; budget legal |
| Security deposit cap | Cash-flow timing | Statutory limit, not “one month + last + extra” folklore |
| Habitability / mold / old electrical | Stops rent collection | Inspection + sewer scope + roof + electrical on every 2–4 unit |
| HOA rental cap | Condo hack fails | Read CC&Rs and rental addendum |
| Insurance (FAIR Plan / condo master) | Payment shock | Quote landlord + HO-6 *before* removing investigation contingency |
| ADU construction overrun | Eats the “low cash” thesis | Prefer **already permitted and COed** ADU |
| JADU shared-bath occupancy | Forces you to stay on site | Separate sanitation if you want a later full rental |

## Deal flow and “insane deals”

| Roadblock | Why it kills the plan | Pre-address |
|---|---|---|
| Coastal SFR under market | Not a first-time, low-cash product | Hunt 2–4 unit and inland-county legal ADU |
| Auction / probate timing | You cannot use slow DPA easily | Cash or hard money only if you have it; otherwise skip |
| Subject-to / skinny wholesales | Title, due-on-sale, licensing | Attorney or do not touch |
| Scraped “off-market” spam | Trespass / TCPA / CFAA risk | Public records + your agent’s MLS. See LEGAL_AND_ETHICS.md |
| Analysis paralysis | No close | Cap at 10 fully underwritten deals then offer |

## Life

| Roadblock | Why it kills the plan | Pre-address |
|---|---|---|
| Roommate / tenant conflict while you live on site | You move out early and break occupancy | Screen hard; house rules in the lease |
| Job change out of region at month 4 | Occupancy exception is narrow | Document; talk to servicer *before* leaving |
| Rate and MIP | FHA MIP often life-of-loan if <10% down | Model refinance later; do not assume year-1 refi |

# Disclaimer

This repository is educational planning material, not legal, tax, lending, or investment advice.

- Programs, income limits, loan limits, and funding status change without notice. Confirm with a CalHFA-approved or SDHC-participating lender and a California real-estate attorney before acting.
- Occupancy misrepresentation on an FHA, VA, conventional owner-occupied, or down-payment-assistance loan is mortgage fraud.
- Web collection in this project is limited to official sources, public records, and sites that permit automated access. Do not add scrapers that violate robots.txt or site terms.
- California landlord-tenant law, rent control (Costa-Hawkins / local ordinances), habitability, and ADU rules are jurisdiction-specific.
- Section 121 capital-gains exclusion generally requires 24 months of ownership and use as a principal residence in the 5 years before sale. A 12-month stay usually does **not** qualify.

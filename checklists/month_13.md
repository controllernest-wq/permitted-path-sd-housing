# Month-13 conversion checklist

- [ ] 12 months of occupancy evidence: utilities, DL, tax address, insurance
- [ ] Servicer notified only as required; do not invent a story
- [ ] HO-3 converted to landlord policy the week you leave
- [ ] Security deposit handled under CA Civil Code
- [ ] Lease ≥ 31 days; no STR on ADU/JADU
- [ ] If a silent second exists: read whether move-out accelerates it **before** you sign a lease on your old unit
- [ ] Schedule E / depreciation set up with a CPA
- [ ] Maintenance reserve still funded
- [ ] If buying another owner-occupied home: new occupancy will be real too

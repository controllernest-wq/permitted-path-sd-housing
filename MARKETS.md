## Las Vegas metro

**Search:** Las Vegas, Henderson, North Las Vegas, Enterprise, Spring Valley, Paradise, Sunrise Manor. Stretch only if you will actually live there 12 months (not a weekend address).

**Capital stack**
- FHA 3.5% on 1–4 units.
- Home Is Possible: published variants include up to ~4–5% DPA, 1–4 units, occupy in 60 days, 640 FICO. Some products are 0% deferred 30-year seconds (repaid at sale/refi); some are 3–7 year forgivable. Read the **exact second** your lender locks.
- Seller credits up to FHA cap.
- USDA only on the rural fringe — useful if the map says eligible, not as the core Vegas play.

**Property to hunt**
- Legal duplex first (easiest FHA, no 3–4 unit self-sufficiency test).
- Then triplex/fourplex under FHA 3–4 unit limits if rents clear the test.
- Avoid STR-first condos. Occupancy + many HOAs + Clark County STR rules will fight a “Airbnb the other side” plan.

**Honest math pattern (illustrative, not a quote)**
A ~$475k duplex, ~$1,400 on the other unit, FHA PITI+MIP in the mid-$3ks leaves **housing cost in the $2,000s** after one rent — cheaper than many 1-bed leases, not automatically $0. Year 2 (both units rented) is where “close to free” can appear if you bought right and reserved for repairs.

**Occupancy trap**
Forgiveness ≠ permission to leave. If you move at month 13, budget to **repay leftover DPA** from cash or a refi. That is still cheaper than 20% down.

Official starting points
- https://www.homeispossiblenv.org/
- https://housing.nv.gov/
- HUD FHA limits lookup

## Phoenix metro

**Search:** Phoenix, Mesa, Glendale, Peoria, Avondale, Tolleson, Maryvale, west/central corridors first. Scottsdale/Gilbert only if the cap rate still works. Pinal (Maricopa city, Casa Grande) is adjacent — treat as optional only if you will live there.

**Capital stack**
- FHA 3.5%.
- Home in Five Advantage (Maricopa only): assistance often cited up to **5–6%** of loan, 640 FICO, income caps ~$140k–$157k depending on matrix year. Forgivable vs repayable options exist.
- Home Plus (statewide): ~4%, higher income cap (~$155k in 2026 snapshots), duplex allowed on some first-lien products, **3–4 units excluded** in the April 2026 lender summary.
- City of Phoenix Open Doors: up to 10% for ≤80% AMI **inside Phoenix**, occupy for the life of that assistance — skip if month-13 move-out is the plan.
- National DPA (Chenoa etc.) if income blows local caps.

**Property to hunt**
- Duplex/triplex in central and west Phoenix where list prices still sit under FHA multi-unit limits.
- If using Home Plus, do not fall in love with a fourplex unless the first lien is plain FHA without that DPA.
- ADU / casita: verify CO and zoning. Phoenix is less “ministerial ADU” than coastal CA.

**Honest math pattern**
Marketing slides that show a Phoenix duplex at ~$160/month housing cost assume clean rents and no deferred maintenance. Underwrite 8% vacancy, 8–10% capex on 1960s block, and summer turnover.

Official starting points
- https://homeplusaz.com/
- https://www.homeinfive.com/ (confirm current URL with lender)
- https://www.phoenix.gov/housing

## Palm Springs metro (Coachella Valley)

**Search:** Palm Springs, Cathedral City, Desert Hot Springs, Palm Desert, Indio, La Quinta, Thousand Palms, unincorporated pockets. Indian Wells / Rancho Mirage only if cash flow survives luxury pricing.

**Capital stack**
- FHA to **$690k** 1-unit / **$883k** 2-unit in Riverside County.
- CalHFA MyHome (1-unit emphasis; ADU/granny may ride with a 1-unit).
- Riverside County PLHA FTHB: up to **20% / $100k**, 120% AMI, purchase-price caps (~$607k SFR / ~$470k condo in the 2026–27 packet). Cities listed include Palm Springs. These are **occupancy-tied** silent seconds — bad fit for a planned month-13 vacancy unless you can repay.
- City of Palm Springs FTHB (when funded): large seconds (docs have cited up to $200k) aimed at **residents**, income-capped, occupancy-heavy.
- GSFA / national DPA as the flexible layer if you will not stay for county forgiveness.

**Property to hunt**
- SFR + legal casita/ADU more than classic duplex inventory.
- Seasonal demand: winter rents can look heroic; summer can sit empty. Underwrite **annual** rent, not February rent.
- No STR as the year-1 plan on FHA/DPA.

**Honest math pattern**
This metro wins if you want to *be* in the desert 12 months and keep CA program access. It loses if the only goal is maximum cash flow per dollar of credit.

Official starting points
- https://www.calhfa.ca.gov/homebuyer/
- https://rivcohws.org/housing-programs
- City of Palm Springs housing / HWS packets

## Decision rule

1. Need the plan to pencil as housing-cost offset in year 1 → **start in Clark County duplexes**.
2. Need more buildings to look at and strong % DPA → **Maricopa**, but match DPA product to unit count.
3. Need CA + winter market and will accept weaker cash flow → **Coachella Valley**, and do **not** attach occupancy-for-life county seconds if you will leave at month 13.

FHA occupancy does not change by state: move in ~60 days, live there ~12 months, other legal units may rent immediately.

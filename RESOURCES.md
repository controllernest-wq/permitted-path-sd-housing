# Resources — Las Vegas, Palm Springs, Phoenix

Verify on the official page. Funding and matrices change.

## All three

- HUD FHA limits: https://entp.hud.gov/idapp/html/hicostlook.cfm
- FHA Handbook 4000.1 occupancy (60 days / 12 months intent)
- HUD counseling: https://www.hud.gov/program_offices/housing/sfh/hcc
- IRS Pub 523 / Section 121 (24-month use for exclusion)

## Las Vegas metro (Clark County, NV)

- Nevada Housing Division: https://housing.nv.gov/
- Home Is Possible: https://www.homeispossiblenv.org/
- Nevada Rural Housing (fringe / some Clark rural addresses): https://nvrural.org/programs/homeownership/
- Clark County assessor / recorder for unit count and deeds
- GLVAR market stats via a local broker

## Phoenix metro (Maricopa County, AZ)

- Home Plus (AZ IDA): https://homeplusaz.com/
- Home in Five (Maricopa / Phoenix IDAs): ask a participating lender for the live portal
- City of Phoenix Open Doors DPA fact sheet (80% AMI, occupy for program term)
- Maricopa County assessor
- Arizona Residential Landlord and Tenant Act

## Palm Springs metro (Riverside County, CA)

- CalHFA homebuyer: https://www.calhfa.ca.gov/homebuyer/
- CalHFA MyHome: https://www.calhfa.ca.gov/homebuyer/programs/myhome.htm
- Riverside County HWS housing programs: https://rivcohws.org/housing-programs
- City of Palm Springs / county PS FTHB packets when funded
- GSFA Platinum: https://www.gsfahome.org/programs/dpa/platinum.shtml
- Riverside County assessor; city building permits for casitas

## Still useful nationally

- Chenoa Fund and similar no-income-limit FHA DPA when local income caps bind
- HUD Home Store REO

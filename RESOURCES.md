# Valuable resources (San Diego + CA + national)

Verify every number on the official page before you apply. Funding windows open and close.

## Official housing finance

- CalHFA homebuyer programs: https://www.calhfa.ca.gov/homebuyer/
- CalHFA MyHome: https://www.calhfa.ca.gov/homebuyer/programs/myhome.htm
- CalHFA approved lenders: search from calhfa.ca.gov
- San Diego Housing Commission FTHB: https://sdhc.org/housing-opportunities/first-time-homebuyers/
- County of San Diego housing programs (DCCA and related): via SDHC county pages and county housing resources
- HUD approved housing counseling: https://www.hud.gov/program_offices/housing/sfh/hcc
- FHA Handbook 4000.1 (occupancy, 1–4 unit): HUD.gov
- FHA mortgage limits: https://entp.hud.gov/idapp/html/hicostlook.cfm
- FHFA conforming limits: https://www.fhfa.gov/data/conforming-loan-limit

## Down payment assistance directories (use as maps, then confirm official)

- Down Payment Scout — San Diego: https://downpaymentscout.com/down-payment-assistance-san-diego/
- GSFA Platinum: https://www.gsfahome.org/programs/dpa/platinum.shtml
- Chenoa Fund: https://chenoafund.org/
- National Homebuyers Fund / similar national DPA — lender overlay dependent

## City of San Diego development / ADU (permitted units only)

- ADU / JADU Information Bulletin 400: https://www.sandiego.gov/development-services/forms-publications/information-bulletins/400
- Development Services portal: https://www.sandiego.gov/development-services
- Permit / record search before you believe a listing’s “in-law suite”

## Tax

- IRS Publication 523 (selling your home / Section 121)
- IRC 121 two-of-five year rule; 121(b)(5) nonqualified use
- Franchise Tax Board — California conforms to 121 but taxes remaining gain at ordinary state rates
- Talk to a CPA **before** converting to rental or selling

## Public records and distressed (legal deal flow)

- San Diego County Assessor / Recorder / Clerk: https://arcc.sdcounty.ca.gov/
- San Diego County Treasurer-Tax Collector (tax default information is public)
- HUD Homes: https://www.hudhomestore.gov/
- US Bankruptcy PACER (advanced; fee-based)
- Court probate indexes (county)

## Landlord compliance (when you convert)

- California Civil Code deposit and habitability provisions
- HCD / local rent program for the specific city
- California Association of Realtors lease forms via your broker
- Housing providers’ insurance (DP-3 vs HO-3 vs landlord)

## Market data (research, not offers)

- San Diego Association of Realtors statistics
- Census ACS for tract-level rents and income
- HUD FMR documents for a conservative rent floor

## Education

- eHome California (CalHFA-accepted online counseling in many files)
- BiggerPockets house-hacking library — strategy literacy, not underwriting
- Local REIA only as a filter; ignore “subject-to get rich” pitches

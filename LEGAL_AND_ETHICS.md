# Legal and ethics guardrails

## Occupancy

FHA (HUD Handbook 4000.1) requires at least one borrower to occupy within 60 days and to intend to occupy for at least one year. FHA will not insure a loan designed as a stealth investment property.

Living in unit A while renting units B–D is contemplated by the program. Never occupying, or leaving a suitcase, is not.

## Down payment assistance notes

Read the **note and deed of trust you sign**. Program marketing is not the contract.

- SDHC / County DCCA style programs: non-occupancy typically accelerates the silent second.
- GSFA Platinum FAQ (as published): no required remaining occupancy period after purchase and initial residency; still must be originated as a primary residence.
- Forgivable seconds: forgiveness is usually a schedule plus occupancy plus on-time first-lien payments.

## Scraping and the agent

Allowed in this project:

- Official government pages and published program PDFs
- County assessor / recorder public data where automation is permitted
- HUD Home Store and similar public inventories
- CSV / export your licensed agent is allowed to give you
- Sites that publish an API or explicit open license

Not allowed:

- Circumventing Zillow, Redfin, Realtor.com, or MLS robots.txt / terms
- Harvesting emails/phones for cold spam (TCPA / CAN-SPAM)
- Login walls, CAPTCHA farms, or credential stuffing
- Representing the bot as a licensed agent

## Construction and rentals

- No rental of a unit that lacks a certificate of occupancy / required permits.
- ADU/JADU minimum lease 31 days in San Diego rulesets cited in IB-400.
- Contractor must be licensed for work that requires it (CSLB).

## Advice boundary

Nothing here is a loan commitment, a legal opinion, or a guarantee of cash flow.
